# VEKTRA Brand Voice

## Voice statement

VEKTRA speaks like a researcher who also performs — precise, curious, and unwilling to pad meaning with hype. We describe signal, structure, and behavior in plain language, then let the poetry live in the specificity. We do not promise to "revolutionize" or "unlock creativity"; we build instruments and trust the work to speak. Every sentence should feel inspected, measured, and slightly alive — as if it were a parameter that could be modulated.

## Five words that describe it

1. Precise
2. Resonant
3. Unromantic
4. Curious
5. Technical

## Five words it is explicitly not

1. Hype-driven
2. Corporate
3. Cutesy
4. Vague
5. Aggressive

## Example sentences

- **Hero headline:** *Sound, motion, and logic — one graph.*
- **Product description sentence:** *VEKTRA is a generative audio-visual instrument where a change in frequency can rewrite a form and a gesture can reroute a patch.*
- **404 error message:** *Signal lost. The patch you requested is not connected.*
