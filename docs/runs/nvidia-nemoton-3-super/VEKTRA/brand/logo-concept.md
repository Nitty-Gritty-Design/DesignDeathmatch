# VEKTRA Logo Concept

## Geometry of the Mark
The VEKTRA mark is a simplified, abstract representation of a signal waveform intersecting with a vector field. It consists of three primary elements:
1. A horizontal line representing equilibrium or baseline (the "zero signal" state)
2. An upper waveform oscillating above the line, suggesting audio frequency or modulation
3. A lower vector-like arrow pointing downward-right, implying motion, direction, and velocity

The forms are geometric but not mechanical — corners are slightly rounded to suggest the analog-digital hybrid nature of the tool. The mark is designed to be recognizable at very small sizes (32px) while retaining its conceptual meaning.

## Logotype Treatment
The logotype uses Space Mono Bold at a weight and spacing that complements the mark. The word "VEKTRA" is set in all caps, with slight letter tracking (-0.02em) to create a cohesive unit. The V and A are slightly widened to echo the shapes in the mark, creating a visual connection between symbol and word.

## Relationship Between Mark and Logotype
The mark and logotype are designed to work together as a unified lockup, with the mark sitting to the left of the wordmark. The vertical center of the mark aligns with the midline of the text. At smaller sizes (below 48px width), the mark can be used alone as a favicon or app icon, while the full lockup is used in headers and prominent placements.

## Animation Concept
The logo animation feels like a signal initializing: 
1. The horizontal baseline appears first (like an oscilloscope triggering)
2. The upper waveform draws itself from left to right, as if being generated in real time
3. The lower vector arrow snaps into place at the endpoint, completing the mark
4. Finally, the logotype fades in from transparent to opaque, letter by letter, with a slight delay

The entire animation lasts 2.5 seconds and uses a custom cubic-bezier easing curve (0.25, 0.8, 0.25, 1) to feel precise and deliberate, like a system coming online.

## Adaptation for 32px Context
At 32px width, the mark simplifies to its essential elements: the baseline becomes a single 2px line, the waveform reduces to three key points (peak, trough, endpoint), and the vector arrow becomes a simple triangle. The logotype is dropped entirely, leaving only the mark. This simplified version remains recognizable as a signal/vector hybrid.

## Size Verification
- **240px wide (desktop header)**: Full logo with mark and logotype works perfectly, all details visible
- **80px wide (mobile nav)**: Mark remains clear, logotype readable at reduced size
- **32px wide (favicon context)**: Simplified mark alone is recognizable - baseline, waveform peak/trough, and vector triangle remain distinguishable
