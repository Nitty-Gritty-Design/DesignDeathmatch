# Voice — VEKTRA

## The voice

VEKTRA speaks the way a sound engineer describes a patch — with precision, with the assumption that you can keep up, and without apologizing for the technical vocabulary. The tone is calm, declarative, and slightly literary. It is never breathless, never corporate, never aspirational in the SaaS sense. It treats the reader as a peer who builds things. It uses short sentences, often. It allows itself the occasional sharp metaphor. When in doubt it picks the word that means exactly the thing, not the word that flatters the brand.

## Five words that describe it

- **Precise**
- **Tactile**
- **Quietly confident**
- **Technical**
- **Aloof, in a good way**

## Five words it is explicitly not

- Friendly
- Disruptive
- Revolutionary
- Empowering
- Seamless

## Three example sentences

**Hero headline (≤ 7 words):**
> Sound, motion, and code as one graph.

**Product description (one sentence):**
> VEKTRA is a live environment where audio, visual, and logical signals share a single timebase, so a change in frequency can drive a change in geometry, and a gesture can rewrite a patch.

**404 error message:**
> The signal was lost. Check the cable. Check the time. Check the path.
