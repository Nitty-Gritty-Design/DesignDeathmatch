# VEKTRA V2 Logo Concept & Design Brief

This document details the redesigned **Version 2** logo system, built to communicate the premium, complex synthesis of sound and spatial mathematics.

---

## 1. Geometry of the Mark (V2)
The V2 mark moves beyond the single line wave of V1 to introduce a **generative vector lattice and phase interference grid**.

- **The Primary Vector V**: A bold, sharp asymmetrical V-form running on a 45-degree angle.
- **The Interference Waves**: Surrounding the right arm of the V is a series of four concentric, ultra-thin bezier curves representing audio wave propagation, dispersion, and phase modulation.
- **Precision Crosshairs & Markers**: Tiny tick marks and coordinate indicators (`X:01`, `Y:95`, `GRID LOCK: 440Hz`) are integrated directly into the graphic, mimicking the readouts of a high-end hardware vector analyzer.
- **The Active Node**: A double-concentric circle representing a glowing oscilloscope electron beam source at the vertex of the wave path.

---

## 2. Typographic Logotype (V2)
- **Wordmark**: `VEKTRA` in uppercase Space Grotesk.
- **Styling**: Configured with a lighter font weight (`500`) and a wider letter-spacing (`0.24em`), achieving a highly refined, premium museum-archive typographic aesthetic.

---

## 3. Relationships & Variants
- **Horizontal Combination (Primary)**: The complex vector mark sits on the left, separated by a thin, glowing technical divider line.
- **Standalone Mark**: Just the vector grid mark and coordinate ticks, centered inside a square canvas.
- **Colors**:
  - Dark Mode: Glowing emerald-cyan (`#00e65c`), coordinate blue-cyan (`#00d4ff`), and slate off-white (`#f8fafc`).
  - Light Mode: Muted forest-green (`#00993d`), ocean-blue (`#007a99`), and dark slate (`#0f172a`).

---

## 4. Boot Sequence Animation (V2)
The boot animation (`v2/brand/logo-animated.html`) is designed to look like a high-performance vector oscilloscope monitor booting up:
1. **Focus Beam (0.0s - 0.6s)**: The central active node dot blinks rapidly, leaving a residual glow using a CSS filter blur.
2. **Trace Drawing (0.6s - 1.8s)**: The sharp vector lines of the V trace themselves outward.
3. **Lattice Reveal (1.8s - 2.8s)**: The wave dispersion lines fade in with a high-frequency grid-flicker keyframe, simulating electron gun alignment.
4. **Data Lock (2.8s - 3.4s)**: The tiny text coordinate ticks and divider line blink into place, followed by the smooth fade-in of the logotype letters.
